AI-Based Waste Reporting & Cleanliness Verification System

A comprehensive technical overview of the computer vision pipeline, automated spatial dispatch algorithms, and photographic verification engine.

YOLOv8 & OpenCV

The system integrates real YOLOv8 nano convolutional weights for rapid object localization. Images are preprocessed using OpenCV (resizing, color space transformations, Laplacian variance sharpness auditing) before running bounding-box inference.

  • Multi-class recognition (plastic, organic, hazardous, debris).
  • Real-time HUD statistics & confidence scoring.
  • Modular architecture supporting custom waste weights.

Smart Ward Allotment

Uses Haversine spherical distance and geofenced municipal zones to assign citizen complaints to the nearest municipal ward. Incident categories are deterministically routed to the responsible department.

  • Food & wet waste → Organic Composting Wing
  • Plastic accumulation → Resource Recovery Unit
  • Canal/Drainage waste → Eco-Squad Water Division

Cleanliness Verification

The flagship innovation of the project compares the original citizen photo against the worker's after-cleaning photograph using the verified mathematical reduction formula:

((Before − After) / Before) × 100

Combined with OpenCV ORB feature matching and Canny clutter edge density reduction to verify site authenticity.

Cleanliness Verification Decision Matrix

Verification Status Waste Reduction % OpenCV Clutter & Similarity Recommended Action
Passed ≥ 80.0% reduction Low edge density & high scene correlation Immediate officer approval for complaint closure.
Needs Review 50.0% – 79.9% reduction Borderline debris or minor angle deviation Mandatory officer inspection of composite images before closure.
Failed < 50.0% or waste increased High persistent edge density Automatic rejection; complaint returned to field worker for rework.

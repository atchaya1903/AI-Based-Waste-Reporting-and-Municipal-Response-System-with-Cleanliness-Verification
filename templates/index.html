Intelligent Waste Management Architecture

Combining cutting-edge computer vision with automated municipal dispatch to maintain pristine public spaces.

YOLOv8 Detection

Instantly analyzes uploaded images or live camera frames using trained YOLOv8 neural networks to locate, bound, and classify waste items.

Auto-Ward Routing

Identifies the municipal ward and dispatches tasks to the responsible department (Sanitation, Recycling, Eco-Squad) using GPS geofencing.

Cleanliness Verification

Computes waste reduction percentage:
((Before − After) / Before) × 100 with OpenCV structural clutter analysis.

Officer Governance

Requires municipal officer review before final closure when confidence is borderline, ensuring full civic accountability.

Step-by-Step Resolution

Complete Lifecycle of a Waste Complaint

Step 1
Citizen Report

Citizen snaps garbage photo with camera and drops location pin.

Step 2
AI Detection

YOLOv8 & OpenCV count objects and classify waste category.

Step 3
Ward Allotment

Automatic dispatch to responsible ward & sanitation department.

Step 4
Field Cleanup

Worker navigates to site, initiates cleanup, and sweeps area.

Step 5
After Photo

Worker uploads after-cleaning photo to trigger AI verification.

Step 6
Verified & Closed

AI verifies ≥ 80% waste reduction; officer confirms closure.
